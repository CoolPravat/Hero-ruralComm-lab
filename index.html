03 · Faculty debrief

A defensible four-layer communication stack

This is an illustration, not the answer. Compare its internal logic with your team's own strategy and explain the strategic trade-off.

Ready to compare?

Commit to your four choices before viewing the illustrative solution.

L1 · Vernacular

Chhath-anchored Bhojpuri film: hero = farmer's son returning home on his bike. Dialect, not Hindi dub.

L2 · Creator

Replace Dhoni-only with 40 regional micro-creators (mechanics, farm-vloggers) in Bhojpuri/Maithili + one celebrity halo film.

L3 · Commerce-led

WhatsApp Business catalogue in every dealer's hand + ₹99 UPI “reserve” + ONDC-listed spares. Ad-to-action in one tap.

L4 · Ethics

Publish a five-year cost-of-ownership calculator in regional languages: “total paisa”, not just EMI.

The trade-off: slower celebrity halo, faster village-level trust. Hero chooses depth over reach.

05 · Critical reflection

After developing, audit your strategy

Move from thinking with AI, to human audit, to thinking about AI. The distinction between an accurate calculation and a genuinely fair rural buying experience is central.

01

Thinking with AI

How could an interactive AI tool (e.g., a WhatsApp voice-calculator) be deployed to help first-time rural buyers calculate honest, transparent five-year total cost of ownership (TCO) in their own dialect?

02

Human Audit

What economic realities—informal mechanic labour rates, fluctuating monsoon crop cycles, local counterfeit parts, etc.—could this algorithm miss or distort? How should human experts audit the tool so that “mathematical accuracy” does not mask practical rural irrelevance?

03

About AI

How would you prevent data from this bot being weaponised by fintech lenders to price-discriminate or push credit? Where is the line between genuine consumer empowerment and predatory digital profiling?
